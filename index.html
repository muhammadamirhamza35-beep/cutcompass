What it reads

Six things it measures

Every number in the plan comes from one of these readings, or from the details you pick in the form.

Pause map

Measures loudness every 20 ms against the clip's own noise floor and lists every silence over half a second with its timecode.

Frame colors

Samples the wall behind the speaker and the outfit in front, then checks caption contrast against both.

Light and exposure

Reads brightness, contrast, clipped highlights and color cast to suggest exposure and white-balance fixes.

Speaking pace

Counts syllable peaks per second of speech and the share of pauses to tell slow, normal and fast talkers apart.

Format

Reads resolution, aspect ratio and length to choose the platform preset, caption safe zones and structure.

SFX matching

Sorts your library by file and folder names, then picks files for each sound cue in the plan, with a play button.

Your footage never leaves your computer

CutCompass uses your browser's own video and audio decoders. Nothing is uploaded or stored, so it's safe to use with unreleased client footage. Close the tab and every trace of the file is gone.

FAQ

Questions editors ask

Which files work?

MP4 (H.264 with AAC audio), WebM and most MOV files work in Chrome and Edge. Some iPhone HEVC clips don't decode in every browser. If frames don't load, export a quick H.264 proxy, or drop an MP3 or WAV of the audio to still get the pause map.

How accurate is the pause detection?

It measures loudness every 20 ms and compares it with the clip's own noise floor, so it adapts to quiet studios and noisy rooms. It works best on a clean voice. Music or crowd noise under the voice makes pauses harder to find, and the tool tells you when that happens.

Why didn't one of my SFX files match?

Matching reads file and folder names. "USFX - Stomp - 4.wav" is sorted as an impact, but "ui-sounds-pack-2-sound-2.mp3" can't be. Rename unclear files with a word like whoosh, click, riser, impact or ding and add the folder again.

Is there a file size limit?

Frame sampling works on any size. Audio analysis reads the whole file into memory, so it's limited to 900 MB. For longer recordings, export the audio as MP3 or WAV and add it with the "Add audio file" button.

Does it edit the video for me?

No. It plans the edit and gives you a cut list with timecodes. You make the cuts in Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut Pro or CapCut.

CutCompass combines measurements from your file with rule-based editing guidance. Treat the plan as a strong starting point and trust your eyes and ears in the final edit.
