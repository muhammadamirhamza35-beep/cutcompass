# 🧭 CutCompass

Know every cut, sound and color before you edit.

CutCompass is a free, single-page tool that turns your talking-head footage details into a complete edit plan.

## What you get

- **Cuts and transitions**: jump cuts, punch-ins, J/L-cuts, B-roll timing
- **Foley and SFX**: sounds that fit your video type
- **Ambient sound**: room tone and levels for your location
- **Music and mix**: genre, BPM and dB levels under the voice
- **Text colors**: a palette with hex codes that fits your brand and background
- **Color grade**: temperature, contrast, saturation and lighting fixes
- **Structure and export**: hook timing, chapters and platform export settings

## How it works

1. Choose your video type, mood, location, lighting, platform, length and speaker pace.
2. Optionally add your brand color, background color and model's dress color.
3. Click **Build edit plan** and copy the result.

## Run it

It is one static file with no build step. Open `index.html` in a browser, or host it with GitHub Pages.

## Note

CutCompass gives rule-based guidance from the details you enter. It does not analyze your video file, so treat the plan as a strong starting point and use your own creative judgment.
